# Subscribe account to an additional plan

POST {{baseUrl}}/accounts/:accountId/plans/addons


<p>
Subscribe a subaccount to a Zoom add-on plan. 
</p>



<p>
Only master accounts that pay all billing charges of their associated Pro or higher sub accounts can use this API.
</p>



<p>
Zoom only allows approved partners to use 
<a href="https://marketplace.zoom.us/docs/api-reference/master-account-apis">
Master APIs
</a>
 and to manage billing information for  subaccounts. Email the partner programs team at 
<a href="mailto:partner-success@zoom.us">

<strong>
partner-success@zoom.us
</strong>

</a>
 for more details. 
</p>



<p>

<strong>
Prerequisites:
</strong>
 
* A Pro or a higher plan with the 
<strong>
Master account
</strong>
 option enabled. 
* The subaccount 
<strong>
must
</strong>
 be a paid account whose billing charges are paid by its Master account.
</p>



<p>

<strong>
Scopes:
</strong>
 
<code>
billing:master
</code>

</p>



<p>

<strong>

<a href="https://marketplace.zoom.us/docs/api-reference/rate-limits#rate-limits">
Rate Limit Label
</a>
:
</strong>
 
<code>
HEAVY
</code>

</p>
