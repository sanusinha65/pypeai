# Add peering phone numbers

POST {{baseUrl}}/phone/peering/numbers


<p>
Adds phone numbers to Zoom through the Provider Exchange.
</p>



<p>

<strong>
Note
</strong>
: Phone peering API and events are for partners who have completed the MoU to peer with Zoom. To become a peering provider/ carrier, submit your 
<a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSewkY6ixVyKVNkWC-vgmejC16gigxsJWXji3dWzE3XlWtjsgg/viewform">
request
</a>
.
</p>



<p>

<strong>
Prerequisites:
</strong>
 
* A Business or Enterprise account 
* A Zoom Phone license
</p>



<p>

<strong>
Scopes:
